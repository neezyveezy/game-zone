3Privacy Policy

Short version: Game Zone doesn't collect your personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, no ads, no tracking or analytics, and no cookies set by Game Zone. Your game progress stays on your own device.

What Game Zone does not collect

NeezyVeezy does not collect, receive, store, sell or share your name, email address, location, contacts or any other personal information. Nothing you do in the games is sent to NeezyVeezy.

What is saved on your device

To remember your progress and settings, the games use your browser's built-in storage ("local storage" and, for BEATSTORM, the browser's built-in database). This information stays in your browser on your device and is never sent anywhere. It includes things like:

  • Game progress: credits, items, levels, unlocked content and high scores.
  • Settings: sound and music on/off, volume, chosen tracks, quality options and seasonal decorations.
  • Small extras: your last played game, which news you've already seen, and the in-game names you type (like a callsign).
  • BEATSTORM song library: the music files you add and the note charts made for them, so you don't have to add them again. Your music is never uploaded anywhere.

You're in control of this data. To delete it, clear the site data for this website in your browser's settings. That erases all progress and settings for Game Zone and its games on that device.

Seasonal events use your device's date

Holiday events (like Halloween or Christmas) are based on the date and time of your own device. This is checked inside your browser and is not sent anywhere.

Pixel Front multiplayer

Multiplayer only starts when you choose 🌐 Multiplayer and host or join a room. Then:

  • Your browser connects directly to the other players' browsers (peer-to-peer, using WebRTC). Game moves and chat go straight between the players and are not sent to or stored by NeezyVeezy.
  • To find each other, the browsers briefly use the free PeerJS server, which sees the room code and the technical connection details (like your IP address).
  • Because the connection is direct, the other players in the room can see your IP address, as in most online games. Only play multiplayer with people you know and trust.
  • The name and color you pick are shown to the other players in the room. Your name is also saved on your own device for next time.
  • Chat messages are only shown during the game and are not saved anywhere. Be kind in chat.
