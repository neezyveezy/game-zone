PHOTOSENSITIVITY WARNING

BEATSTORM has festival light shows: flashing lights, strobe effects and fast-moving colored beams that pulse to the music.

A small number of people may have a seizure when they see certain flashing lights or patterns, even if they have never had one before. If you or anyone in your family has epilepsy, talk to a doctor before playing.

Stop playing right away and rest if you feel dizzy, have blurred vision, eye or muscle twitches, or feel confused or unwell.

HOW TO PLAY

1 · ADD YOUR MUSIC

Pick Solo or 2 Players, then press ＋ Add Songs (or drag music files onto the list). MP3, WAV, OGG and M4A all work.

BEATSTORM listens to each song and builds the notes automatically: it finds the tempo, the beats, the drum hits and the melody.

Your songs are saved in your browser, so they'll still be there next time. Hover a song to hear a preview.

2 · HIT THE NOTES

  • Press a lane's key when its note reaches the strike line at the bottom.
  • Easy / Medium / Hard use 4 lanes. Expert uses 5.
  • Long notes: hold the key until the tail ends.
  • Timing: PERFECT, GREAT, GOOD or MISS.

3 · STREAKS, FIRE & OVERDRIVE

  • Every 10 notes in a row raises your multiplier, up to x4.
  • Streaks of 50 and 100 set your highway on fire 🔥, and it changes color every 100 notes after that, up to rainbow fire at 1000.
  • Hit every note in a glowing white phrase to charge Overdrive.
  • At 50% or more, activate Overdrive to double your multiplier (up to x8).

4 · PRACTICE & LYRICS

  • Practice: slow a song down, pick a section and loop it until you nail it.
  • Lyrics: shown at the top while you play. They come from your song file, a .lrc file, or the 🎤 AI that listens to the singing.

DEFAULT KEYS

Player 1: A S D F (+G) · Overdrive SPACE

Player 2: J K L ; (Expert: H J K L ;) · Overdrive ENTER

ESC pauses. All keys can be changed in Settings.

🌐 ONLINE BATTLE

  • One player presses Create room and shares the 5-letter code. The others type it into Join.
  • 1v1, 1v1v1 and 1v1v1v1 are everyone for themselves. In 2v2 your score adds up with your teammate's.
  • Match types: Normal, ⚔️ Battle (items) and 🏅 Ranked (1v1 only, changes your rating).
  • The host picks the song. Everyone needs their own copy of the song file, because BEATSTORM never sends music to anyone. The host's game sends the notes, so everyone plays the same chart.
  • Pick your difficulty, press READY, and the host starts the countdown. You see everyone's score live, and the best score wins.

🎆 FESTIVAL LIGHTS

Every song gets its own light show, timed to its beats: moving beams, lasers, a crowd, and flashes, strobes, pyro and confetti when the song drops.

Change it in Settings → Lights: Full, Calm (no strobes or flashes) or Off.
