HOW TO PLAY

1 · ADD YOUR MUSIC

Pick Solo or 2 Players, then press ＋ Add Songs (or drag music files onto the list). MP3, WAV, OGG and M4A all work.

BEATSTORM listens to each song and builds the notes automatically: it finds the tempo, the beats, the drum hits and the melody. This takes a few seconds per song.

Your songs are saved in your browser, so they'll still be there next time.

2 · HIT THE NOTES

  • Press a lane's key when its note reaches the strike line at the bottom.
  • Easy / Medium / Hard use 4 lanes. Expert uses 5.
  • Long notes: hold the key until the tail ends.
  • Timing: PERFECT, GREAT, GOOD or MISS.

3 · STREAKS & OVERDRIVE

  • Every 10 notes in a row raises your multiplier, up to x4.
  • Hit every note in a glowing white phrase to charge Overdrive.
  • At 50% or more, activate Overdrive to double your multiplier (up to x8).
  • Earn up to 5 stars. Beat the star targets with Overdrive to get gold stars.
