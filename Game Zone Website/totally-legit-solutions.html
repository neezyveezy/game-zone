    Day one on the phones

    Your manager, Mr. Gristle, wants $1,500 in gift cards by 5 PM. Hit the quota and you come back tomorrow with a higher one. Miss it and you're fired, and your save starts over. Everyone on your list has their own personality. They react to what you say, ask you questions, and decide whether they trust you.

    1. Walk to Desk 14 and sit down. Arrows or WASD to walk, Space to jump, E to use things.
    2. Call someone from the Call List. Say who you are and tell your story. Script has stories you can use.
    3. They will doubt you. Answer each doubt properly, and keep your lies straight: they remember your name, company, ID and amounts.
    4. Once they run out of questions, ask them to pay. The more convinced they are, the better the method they accept: cash by mail and gift cards are easy, a MoneyGlam wire or crypto pays more, bank details pay the most.
    5. Type the code into Cash Out to get paid. On a break? Try the Arcade, but watch out for Mr. Gristle.

    Tip: coworkers at the water cooler know what each caller likes. The mic button works in Chrome and Edge.
